## Typography

- Display: **Orbitron** (700/900) — uppercase, tracking 0.06–0.14em
- Mono/labels: **Share Tech Mono** — uppercase metadata, terminal voice
- Two voices: Orbitron performs, Share Tech Mono recedes (terminal chrome)

## Texture & Mood

Retro-terminal cyberpunk + playful cartoon mascot ("Minions energy").
Scanlines, radial glows, neon grid floors, starfields, ghost oversized type.
Dark canvas always; accents at full saturation.

## Mascot ("Stander")

Round charcoal body (#1a2026, outline #07090c), one giant green eye
(cream sclera #f2f4e6, green iris, dark pupil, white highlight), green leaf
sprout on curved stem, stubby arms/feet, cream smile. Gold `$` sparkles when
celebrating. Never redraw off-model — use the provided SVG poses.

## Don'ts

- No purple-gradient-on-white, no flat gray neutrals (tint toward navy/green)
- No fonts outside Orbitron / Share Tech Mono
- Never claim the game's points are real StandX Engage Points
- Brand mark is "STANDX COMMUNITY" (community-made, unofficial)
